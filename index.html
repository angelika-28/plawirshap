Delivery you can count on

Same day across town

Order before 2 pm and your flowers arrive by evening. Later orders go out the next morning.

One flat fee

Delivery is a flat $6, and free when your order is over $60.

A handwritten card

Order by email

Pick flowers from the shop, or tell us what you have in mind. Fill in your details and select the button. Your email app opens with the message ready to send. We reply to confirm the total and take payment.

A small shop that buys early

We pick up flowers from the growers' market before sunrise, so what's in the buckets was cut in the last day or two. Every bouquet is tied by hand and wrapped in paper, not plastic.
